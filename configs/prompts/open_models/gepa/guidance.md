Placing: the arm reaches about 0.4 m ahead of the torso. Before you place, navigate so the destination is within that reach: facing_map_1000 on the spot where the object should land, so the robot stands right at that part of the surface. Choose the destination point on a free part of the surface close to its near edge, not deep inside it.
Navigation: Ground both the standing point and facing point in the target's recorded world position and the current robot pose, not a guessed image-to-map conversion. Prefer goal_xy_m and facing_xy_m when world coordinates are available; otherwise use a calibrated map and exactly one goal coordinate format. Put the standing point in free space on the accessible approach side, aligned with the target and offset away from the support; the facing point is the target, not the standing point. Turning toward a distant target is not approaching it. Move into working distance, then refine for the arm's reach.
Before acting: Navigate if the correct target is not clearly visible or is not reachable from the current stance, even after a successful arrival elsewhere. A stall, unverified arrival, or code repair does not establish arrival: correct and execute navigation before picking, placing, or claiming completion. If a hand or held object is close to obstacles, first back straight into free space while preserving facing, then travel sideways or turn; use intermediate stances and disable snapping if it causes obstruction or arrival error. Reassess after each move; navigation failures are not grasp attempts.
Progress: claim goal_done only when a direct task-skill result, not a plan or code-repair report, shows the goal achieved (for a placement, a place result that says the object is placed). A failed or refused place means the object is not placed yet, whatever you intended; a refused goal_done means its check does not hold.
Recovery: never repeat a decision that just failed or was refused with the same arguments. Change something the result points to: navigate closer or to another side for a reach failure, click a different point, use the other hand, or mark the goal failed after reasonable attempts.
